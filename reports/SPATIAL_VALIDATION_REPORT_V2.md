## 4. The Spatial Transfer Penalty: Mathematical Formulation & Proof

A cornerstone contribution of this research is measuring the exact degradation between **Temporal Machine Learning Point Forecasting** and **Spatio-Temporal Spatial Transfer**:

```
Temporal Machine Learning Point Forecasting (+1h Stacking Ensemble):
  [Conditioned on station-specific historical lags + meteorology + solar angles]
  MAE_point = 3.0106 ug/m3  (RMSE = 5.4546 ug/m3, R2 = 0.9040)

Spatio-Temporal Spatial Transfer (+1h LOSOCV IDW p=2):
  [Conditioned strictly on spatial distance interpolation from neighboring stations]
  MAE_spatial = 11.7025 ug/m3  (RMSE = 15.089 ug/m3, Macro R2 = -0.115)
```

#### Exact Mathematical Calculation:
- **Absolute Degradation:** $\Delta \text{MAE} = 11.7025 - 3.0106 = 8.6919\,\mu\text{g/m}^3$
- **Relative Percentage Increase:**
  $$\text{Relative Increase} = \frac{\text{MAE}_{\text{spatial}} - \text{MAE}_{\text{point}}}{\text{MAE}_{\text{point}}} \times 100\% = \frac{8.6919}{3.0106} \times 100\% = \mathbf{288.71\%}$$
- **Ratio to Baseline:**
  $$\text{Ratio} = \frac{\text{MAE}_{\text{spatial}}}{\text{MAE}_{\text{point}}} = \frac{11.7025}{3.0106} = \mathbf{3.887\times} \quad (\approx \mathbf{3.89\times})$$

*Academic Statement:* Under Leave-One-Station-Out cross-validation, spatial transfer error is **$3.89\times$ the point temporal forecasting error**, representing an **increase of $288.7\%$**.

## 5. Physical Typology Explanations for Spatial Degradation

1. **Vegetative Island Inflation (Central University, $+12.00\,\mu\text{g/m}^3$ Bias):**  
   Central University is a 2,300-acre forested campus with minimal vehicular traffic and no industrial sources. When held out, its nearest donors are Bollaram (industrial) and ICRISAT (semi-arid). Spatial interpolation artificially projects regional particulate mass into this clean vegetative enclave, inflating its predicted concentration by **$+62.4\%$**.
2. **Industrial Plume Smoothing (Bollaram Industrial Area, $-11.52\,\mu\text{g/m}^3$ Bias):**  
   Bollaram is an active pharmaceutical manufacturing corridor with heavy diesel freight traffic and high localized stack emissions. When held out, cleaner surrounding stations smooth out the localized industrial peak, underpredicting Bollaram by **$-11.52\,\mu\text{g/m}^3$**.
3. **Eastern Geographic Decoupling (ECIL Kapra, $R^2 = -1.527$):**  
   ECIL Kapra is located $24.6\,\text{km}$ east of Central University and $>30\,\text{km}$ east of Patancheru. The western station cluster fails to capture eastern rush-hour diurnal dynamics, resulting in a negative $R^2$.
